# Preferences

- Look: FeedMe's own design tokens (orange #ff7823, Inter, grey-100 background), read from the public @feedmepos/ui-library package. Orange buttons use near-black text for contrast. Light theme only.
- For: elderly diners (very large text and buttons, one decision per screen) and everyone else at the same table.
- Must have: one QR code per table that offers both menus; no login; no payment screen on the big-photo menu; a confirm step after voice orders.
- Screen text: English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese on both menus; English on the staff page.
- Prices in RM.
